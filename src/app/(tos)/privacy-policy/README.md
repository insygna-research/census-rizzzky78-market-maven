# Privacy Policy

**Route / access:** `/privacy-policy`, reached from the footer's "Legal" section and from links on the Login and Terms of Service pages.
**Part of:** [Project root README](../../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Explains what data Maven collects, how it's used, and what rights users have over it.

## What the user sees

A decorative header graphic above a "Privacy Policy" title and an effective-date note, followed by a ten-item accordion covering: Scope and Purpose, Data We Collect, How We Use Your Data, Data Sharing and Disclosure, Data Security, Data Retention, Your Rights, Third-Party Links, Changes to This Policy, and Contact Us.

## What the user can do

Expand or collapse each accordion section to read it, and use the two buttons at the bottom to jump to **View Terms of Service** or, depending on login state, **Go to Login** / **Go to App**.

## Flow

Reached from the footer or cross-links on Login/Terms of Service. Pairs directly with [Terms of Service](../terms-of-service/README.md).
