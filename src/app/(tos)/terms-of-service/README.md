# Terms of Service

**Route / access:** `/terms-of-service`, reached from the footer's "Legal" section and from links on the Login and Privacy Policy pages.
**Part of:** [Project root README](../../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Sets out the rules and limitations for using Maven, and clarifies that it is a non-commercial, experimental portfolio project.

## What the user sees

A decorative header graphic above a "Terms of Service" title and intro, followed by an eight-item accordion covering: Introduction, Description of Services (including key limitations such as no file attachments and two-product comparison limits), User Responsibilities, Intellectual Property, Experimental Nature, Liability & Warranty, Changes & Termination, and Contact.

## What the user can do

Expand or collapse each accordion section to read it, and use the two buttons at the bottom to jump to **View Privacy Policy** or, depending on login state, **Go to Login** / **Go to App**.

## Flow

Reached from the footer or cross-links on Login/Privacy Policy. Pairs directly with [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/README.md).
