# Cookbook

**Route / access:** `/cookbook`, reached from the "Cookbook" link in the navigation menu and footer.
**Part of:** [Project root README](../../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Teaches visitors how to get the best results from Maven through a structured, guide-book style walkthrough of concepts and step-by-step "recipes."

## What the user sees

A two-column layout: a sticky sidebar table of contents (Introduction, Core Concepts, Recipes, Tips and Tricks, Conclusion, with nested sub-sections) and a scrolling main content area. Core Concepts explains the Orchestrator, Agent Tools, AI-Powered Insights, and Streamed Results. The Recipes section groups seven step-by-step guides into Basic, Advanced, and Expert tabs (e.g. "Personalized Recommendations", "Comparing Two Products", "Combining Multiple Tools"), each recipe listing ingredients, numbered steps, an expected outcome, and troubleshooting tips. Tips and Tricks lists general best practices, and a closing Conclusion card offers a "Go to App" button. A sticky banner also links out to the Maven Webpage v2 showcase.

## What the user can do

Click sidebar entries to jump-scroll to a section, switch between Basic/Advanced/Expert recipe categories and their tabs, and click **Go to App** at the end to open [Chat](../../chat/README.md). On mobile, the sidebar collapses behind a floating toggle button.

## Flow

Reached from the navigation bar or footer on any marketing page. Complements [Features](../features/README.md) and [Workflow](../workflow/README.md); ends by directing the user into the [Chat](../../chat/README.md) app.
