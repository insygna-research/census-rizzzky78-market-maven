# Home

**Route / access:** `/`, the site's landing page — reached directly or via the "MAVEN AI" logo link in the navigation bar.
**Part of:** [Project root README](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Introduces Maven AI to first-time visitors, explains what the assistant does, and drives sign-ups toward the registration flow.

## What the user sees

A full-page marketing scroll: a hero section with the Maven pitch, a sticky banner linking out to a "Maven Webpage v2" showcase, a four-step "How Maven Works" strip (Ask → Think → Smart Actions → Clear Results), a product snapshot showcase, an "Agentic Tools" gallery, a two-column "Why Choose Maven?" benefits list (Save Time, Make Informed Choices, Personalized Experience, AI-Powered Accuracy), a three-card testimonials section, a closing call-to-action banner, and the site footer.

## What the user can do

Scroll through the narrative, follow the sticky banner to the external Maven v2 showcase, and click the **Get Started** button in the closing CTA to go to [/register](<(auth)/register/README.md>). The navigation bar and footer also link to Features, Workflow, Cookbook, Reviews, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.

## Flow

This is the entry point for new visitors. From here users typically continue to [Register](<(auth)/register/README.md>) → [Login](<(auth)/login/README.md>) → [Chat](chat/README.md), or explore the marketing pages ([Features](<(blog)/features/README.md>), [Workflow](<(blog)/workflow/README.md>), [Cookbook](<(blog)/cookbook/README.md>)) first.
